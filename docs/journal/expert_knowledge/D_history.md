## 1. Timeline of knowledge added, changed or removed

**Regimes at a glance.** (1) 10-22→10-29 battle "op engineering": raw engine values in, engine-exact computations (damage, wipe chance, reach, engine-played attacks, Lanchester shares) as graph ops on their own action's logit, contract v1→v16. (2) 10-27 campaign v1: Φ priced by the developer's neutrality rule; NK2's formulas as in-graph ops, explicitly *not* a teacher. (3) 10-28 "priors era": NK2/engine knowledge as fixed priors with bounded, decayed learnable corrections. (4) 10-30 campaign v2: known features joined late; logits as matches; zero-init heads so the first policy is the priors. (5) 10-31→11-11 NK2 teacher era (imitation, DAgger advice, accept sets). (6) 11-04→11-11 knowledge enters mainly as priors (safety, YES, trade, night, towns' night, unseen exits), judged by paired tests from 11-10. (7) 11-11 overhaul: NK2's planner as inputs with bounded "views", teacher loss off, follow mixture, then own games. (8) 11-12 nk-predict head and planner-free experiments on branches. NK2-specific steps are in §6; this table cross-refers to them.
