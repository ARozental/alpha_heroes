**High**
2. **The KL early stop measures against the policy that played, which is about one update old.** The previous update's movement uses up most of this update's KL budget before it takes a step. In this run, 40 updates stopped after a single step and 77 after three or fewer; 54 of those 77 came right after a full update. Each stop also cuts the learning rate by 1.5×, which explains the LR sawtooth. Fix: measure the KL against the model at the start of each update.
